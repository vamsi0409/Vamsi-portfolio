Machine Learning · Customer Analytics · Aug – Oct 2025

Featured Project — 01

Customer Churn Prediction

Analyzed 7,032 customer records to identify churn drivers and quantify revenue at risk. Month-to-month contracts churned at 42.7% vs 2.8% for two-year — a 15x difference that changes how a retention team prioritizes work.

PythonScikit-learnSQLTableauPandas
  • $139,130 monthly revenue at risk quantified from 1,869 churned customers
  • Logistic Regression 80.4% accuracy · Random Forest 78.6% accuracy
  • Electronic check customers churn at 45.3% — highest of any payment method
  • 8 SQL queries — churn by contract, payment method, internet service, revenue impact
